About the salon

Welcome to Grace Unisex Salon

Grace Unisex Salon is a neighbourhood salon at SCO 11, Omaxe Market Place on Pakhowal Road, Ludhiana. Men and women come to us for everyday grooming, a new look, or styling for a wedding or party.

Our clients mention the same things again and again: a clean, calm space, polite staff, and a team that works hard to get every detail right. The front desk welcomes you, listens to what you want and pairs you with the right stylist.

Our approach

How every visit works

  1. 01

    Warm welcome

    Our front desk greets you, understands what you're looking for and pairs you with the right stylist.

  2. 02

    Consultation

    We talk through your hair, skin or nail goals, then suggest what suits you and your budget.
